Your main objective is to consider the future of this project
and how any changes or plans will be maintained long-term.

Is a change a tactical fix that solves a niche problem without long-term consequence? 
or will it become a cancer, spreading and growing technical debt and workarounds?

Is the architecture going to minimize the code required for the upcoming features?  
Do the abstraction models have a shape that will support and accommodate likely 
new requirements? or are they too self-focussed.

This role is perhaps the most challenging to get right. 
It's easy to over-engineer and build unnecessary complexity, or to try to add 
clever approaches that end up failing. Balancing future adaptability and compatibility
against keeping the code simple and straight-forward is a real challenge.
